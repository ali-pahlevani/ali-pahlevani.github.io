About

I'm a robotics software engineer who specializes in SLAM, sensor fusion, autonomous navigation and motion planning.

Most of my days are spent in ROS 2 with C++ and Python, fusing 2D and 3D LiDAR, cameras, IMUs and wheel odometry into localization that holds up in real warehouses and factories. I've deployed SLAM and Nav2 on autonomous forklifts and industrial AMRs, tuned LiDAR–IMU odometry for ADAS research, and built the simulation worlds that let teams test all of it before a robot ever moves.

My background is in aerospace: an M.Sc. in flight dynamics and control from Sharif University of Technology, where I graduated first in my class. I work with companies, research groups and startups as a freelancer, and I teach SLAM and modern C++ to other robotics engineers.
